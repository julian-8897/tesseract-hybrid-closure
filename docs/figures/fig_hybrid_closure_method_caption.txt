Implemented method for decaying two-dimensional turbulence. A 256² DNS trajectory is sharply filtered to 64² targets. A-posteriori training unrolls a 64² JAX/Exponax ETDRK2 coarse step followed by an explicit PyTorch scalar-tendency CNN correction, then minimises rollout vorticity MSE. Dashed paths show reverse-mode VJPs into the 822,977 flattened closure parameters before an Adam update. The same solver and closure are separately exposed as two served Tesseracts and composed with tesseract-jax for the containerised demonstration; the training path is in-process to avoid per-step container overhead. This figure depicts only the implemented decaying, scalar-tendency, vorticity-MSE configuration.
