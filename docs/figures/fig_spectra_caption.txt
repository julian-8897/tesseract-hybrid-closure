Radially binned spectra averaged over all 32 validation seeds, at rollout steps 30, 120 and 500. Top row: energy spectrum E(k), sharing one vertical scale across steps. Bottom row: enstrophy spectrum relative to the filtered-DNS reference, Z(k)/Z(k)_DNS, where unity is exact agreement. The enstrophy ratio is shown rather than the energy ratio because the training objective is a vorticity MSE; per mode Z_q = |q|^2 E_q exactly, and the shared factor largely cancels in a shell-level ratio, so the two panels would have been near-duplicates. Both spectra at every step are in docs/results/spectra-validation.json. Diagnostic only: this figure uses the validation split, and no reported metric, model selection, or test-split result depends on it.
